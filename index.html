ME 313 — DESIGN OF MECHANICAL COMPONENTS

Multifunctional 3D-Printed Wrench Handle

Designed and manufactured a lightweight multifunctional wrench handle for detachable crowfoot wrench heads using FDM additive manufacturing.

<70 gMass requirement
ABSFDM material
6Bit-storage locations

Design & Engineering Process

The design progressed through multiple iterations to improve wrench-head retention, local stiffness, mass, manufacturability, and screwdriver-bit storage. SolidWorks was used for CAD development and ANSYS was used to evaluate structural behavior.

FEA Analysis

Finite element simulations evaluated deformation and stress under torque, combined loading, and three-point bending. The analysis identified high-stress regions around the drive/base area and helped guide geometry changes.

Manufacturing & Testing

The final prototype was manufactured using FDM with ABS and soluble support material. The part was inspected and functionally tested after printing.
